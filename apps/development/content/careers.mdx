# Careers

Seven years of product engineering, mostly front-end-leaning full stack:
planning and mapping software, two fintechs, and a payments processor.

9fin is a debt capital markets platform — news, filings and analytics for
people who trade credit. I build the data-heavy applications that sit on top of
it in Python and React, where the interesting problems are the ones that only
show up at scale: query cost, payload size, and keeping a table honest when
it's holding tens of thousands of rows. Started as a Software Engineer,
promoted to Senior in July 2024.

## Checkout.com

<p className="projects__year">Software Engineer · Apr 2022 — Nov 2023 · Remote</p>

<ul className="projects__stack" aria-label="Tech stack">
  <li>Next.js</li>
  <li>TypeScript</li>
  <li>Tailwind CSS</li>
  <li>MDX</li>
  <li>Storybook</li>
  <li>Vercel</li>
</ul>

Checkout.com processes payments for a good chunk of the internet, and I worked
on the properties around the product rather than the product itself.

I built and shipped the internal component library several of those
user-facing products now sit on — TypeScript over Headless UI and Tailwind,
documented in Storybook, tested with React Testing Library and Jest. I also
rebuilt the docs site end to end in Next.js and Tailwind, which was as much a
content problem as a code one: the MDX pipeline had to be something technical
writers could work in without asking an engineer. Page refactoring, image and
script loading, and a smaller bundle took PageSpeed from the mid-40s to over
90 on most docs pages.

The other half of the job was the marketing site — 600+ pages across six
locales on a headless CMS, leaning on Next.js and Vercel to keep it fast
wherever it was served from.

## TerraQuest

<p className="projects__year">Software Developer · Jul 2019 — Jun 2021 · Birmingham</p>

<ul className="projects__stack" aria-label="Tech stack">
  <li>React</li>
  <li>Gatsby</li>
  <li>GraphQL</li>

</ul>

TerraQuest runs the UK's Planning Portal, which means legacy systems used by
every local authority in the country. I worked on the greenfield replacements:
Gatsby and GraphQL against a headless CMS, deployed on Azure.

Two things outlasted me there. A set of interactive mapping tools that started
as a few-week proof of concept in a small team and went on into production, and
the authentication libraries I built on Azure B2C that ended up in most of the
internal and external products. The component library I put together in
styled-system, from Invision and Zeroheight designs, is still what their apps
are built from.
