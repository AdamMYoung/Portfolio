**ByHand** is a directory of independent shops where everything is handmade —
think Product Hunt for makers. Shops apply with captioned evidence of how their
work is made, and a person reviews every application before it goes live.
Shoppers browse by craft without an account, and sign in to leave a
thumbprint, keep lists, and comment on makers' monthly updates. Makers get
their own analytics, and can pay to fast-track review or take a clearly
labelled featured stall — neither of which ever touches the ranking.

The storefront is Next.js on Vercel with Cache Components, so every public page
is anonymous and cacheable, with per-visitor state fetched on the client. It's
backed by FastAPI, SQLAlchemy and Postgres on Fly, with a second, private admin
API and review dashboard that have no public IPs at all. A typed client is
generated from the API's OpenAPI contract, uploads go straight to Cloudflare R2
with presigned URLs, and every deploy is gated on Playwright journeys and axe
accessibility checks.
