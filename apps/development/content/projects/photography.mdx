My photography site, which stopped being a grid and became a building you walk
around. Each room is an exhibit: wander the corridor with WASD or a thumbstick,
walk up to a piece to read its placard, and leave a heart on the ones you like.
There's a minimap that fills in the rooms you've visited, other visitors
milling about, angled light shafts from the fixtures overhead, and enough
post-processing to make a browser feel like a gallery.

Originals are served from Cloudflare R2 and resized on the fly with Sharp, and
the placards are written from each file's own EXIF — so hanging a new picture
is a case of uploading it.
